Your games come first.

A home screen that feels like a console. Your library, recent games and settings are always close at hand.

Play the way you like.

Even without a connection.

Download your games before heading out. They stay playable offline; your saves sync when you reconnect.

Touch or controller.

Touch controls match each console. Connect a controller and they step aside. You can navigate the whole app with its buttons, too.

Switch screens. Keep your progress.

Start on your phone, continue in your browser. Your saves and play time follow your RetroHub account.

If you have played on both devices, the app lets you choose the save to keep before replacing anything.

Download for Android

  1. Install the app.

    Download the APK from GitHub. Open it on your phone and allow installation if Android asks.

  2. Connect your server.

    Enter its address, then the pairing code shown in RetroHub on the web, or sign in with your password.

  3. Take your games with you.

    Your library appears in the app. Download a game to play it, even offline.

Need a RetroHub server?

RetroHub hosts your library and saves on your own hardware. The app connects to it to access your games; no games are included.
